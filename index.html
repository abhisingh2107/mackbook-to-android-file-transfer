📱 USB Transfer

Move files between your MacBook and Android phone over a USB cable — right in the browser.

Just connect your USB-C cable and follow the tutorial

Easily share files from MacBook to Android and from Android to MacBook. Free, no app, no cloud.

📖 How to use – step by step (click to open)
  1. Turn on Developer optionsOn Android go to Settings → About phone and tap “Build number” 7 times.
  2. Enable USB debuggingSettings → Developer options → turn on “USB debugging”.
  3. Connect the USB-C cablePlug one end into your MacBook and the other into your Android phone. Pull down the notification and choose “File transfer”.
  4. Open this page in Chrome or EdgeSafari and Firefox do not support USB access.
  5. Click “Connect device”Choose your phone from the list in the browser popup.
  6. Tap “Allow” on your phoneApprove the “Allow USB debugging?” prompt (tick “Always allow”).
  7. Android → MacBookOpen folders on the page and click any file. Choose where to save it on your Mac.
  8. MacBook → AndroidOpen the destination folder on the phone, then drag files in or click “Upload files here”.

FAQ

  
How do I transfer files from MacBook to Android? Connect a USB-C cable, click Connect device, allow USB debugging on the phone, then drag your Mac files into the page.

  
How do I transfer files from Android to MacBook? After connecting, open your phone folders on this page and click a file to save it to your Mac.

  
Is it private? Yes. Files go straight over the cable between your devices; nothing is uploaded to any server.

  
Phone not showing up? Quit Android File Transfer, Android Studio, or any running adb (run adb kill-server in Terminal) — they block the browser from using the phone.

  
Supported browsers: Chrome, Edge, Opera on macOS. Safari and Firefox do not support WebUSB.
